# v4.3 ECG release source archive

These are unmodified copies of the source-Mac scripts that built and verified
the frozen v4.3 release. Their SHA256 values are recorded in
`../../experiments/hrv_v4_3/provenance.json`.

They retain historical absolute paths and dependencies on earlier local
research packages. They are provenance, not a portable regeneration command.
For model work, copy the verified complete v4.3 dataset and follow
`../../docs/hrv/WINDOWS_MODEL_HANDOFF.md`. Do not rerun an earlier generator or
publisher to recreate the training labels.
